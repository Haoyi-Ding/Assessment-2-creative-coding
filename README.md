# Assessment-2-creative-coding
1.background
Through the movie Kikujiro's Summer, I found the song Summer. Its beautiful and beautiful song makes me unforgettable. Whenever I feel irritable, this song can always calm me down. Therefore, I plan to disassemble and rewrite this song.

The film "Kikujiro's Summer" tells the story of a little boy in order to find his mother during the summer vacation. he journey with Kikujiro, an eccentric middle-aged man.

The special feature of the song "Summer" is that although the melody is cheerful, it does not only express happiness. With the development of music, listeners may also feel a trace of loneliness and nostalgia.

This emotion echoes the story of the movie: although the little boy did not get the expected results, he gained companionship, care and precious memories on the journey.

2.analyse and disassembled than rewrite
When I disassembled this song, I found that it was divided into 5 parts, and the intro of the song shows a light and bright summer. The main melody is very recognizable, expressing the happy and childlike emotions. In the development stage, there have been emotional changes, becoming gentle and nostalgic. Then the main melody returns, and you can feel happiness and warmth. The ending gradually closes, and the mood is calm and full of memories.
Than extract syllables and rhythms, after that use tunpad to interpret the song, and use different instruments to make the song more harmonious.
